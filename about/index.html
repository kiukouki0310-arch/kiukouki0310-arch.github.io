About Eat Safe Japan

Japan is one of the most rewarding food destinations on earth — and one of the hardest if you are coeliac, vegan, halal or living with a food allergy. Wheat hides in soy sauce, fish stock hides in "vegetable" soup, and polite staff may not know what is in a sauce made elsewhere.

Eat Safe Japan explains how Japanese food and kitchens actually work, so you can ask the right questions and eat with confidence.

How we write

Articles are produced with the help of AI writing tools under editorial guidelines set by a Japan-based editor. We focus on explanation and practical steps, avoid invented personal anecdotes, and never claim that a dish or restaurant is guaranteed safe. Japanese phrases on this site are checked by a native speaker.

Not medical advice

Information here is general guidance for travellers. It is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or dietitian. If you have a severe allergy, always carry your medication.
