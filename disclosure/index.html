Affiliate disclosure & privacy

Affiliate links

Eat Safe Japan participates in affiliate programs, including travel booking services and digital products we create. When you book through some of our links we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we write.

Affiliate tracking

To credit bookings, this site uses an affiliate tool (Travelpayouts Drive) that may turn links to travel partners into tracked links and set cookies when you click them. It does not change the price you pay.

Advertising

We may display ads served by third-party networks. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your ad settings.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies or track you across sites, to see which articles are useful. We do not sell personal data.
